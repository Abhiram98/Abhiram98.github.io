Hi! I am a 2nd year PhD student at the University of Colorado, advised by Dr. Danny Dig. 
Broadly, I am interesting in improving the lives of developers, by attempting to automate tasks that are boring and tedious to them.

At CU Boulder, my research has focused on building tools that help developers improve the code quality (enabling best-practices, splitting large methods, consistently renaming variables). These tools have been built using a combination of IDEs, static analysis, and Large Language Models (LLMs).

Previously, I was a data engineer at Rupeek, a fin-tech company based out of my hometown, Bangalore, India.
